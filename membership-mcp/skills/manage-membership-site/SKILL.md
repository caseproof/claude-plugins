# Manage a membership site

1. If the person has more than one site, list their sites first and confirm which one they mean.
2. Read before you write: look up the member, membership or rule they mean, and show what you found.
3. Before any change (a refund, a cancellation, a new coupon or rule, an edit to a member), say exactly what will change and ask them to confirm.
4. After a change, read it back and tell them it's done in one sentence.
5. If the connector asks them to sign in, they sign in on membershipmcp.ai. Never ask for a password in chat.
